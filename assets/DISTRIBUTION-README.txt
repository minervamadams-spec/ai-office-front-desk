AI Office Front Desk
=====================

What this is
------------
A local-first desktop work desk. Everything you connect and everything you type
stays on this computer — there's no account with us and no server we run.

Installing
----------
1. Move "AI Office Front Desk.app" into your Applications folder (optional, but
   normal for Mac apps).
2. Double-click it to launch.

The first time you open it, macOS will likely say the app "cannot be opened
because it is from an unidentified developer" or "Apple could not verify...".
That's expected — this build isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer
certificate yet, not a sign anything is wrong. To open it anyway:

  - Right-click (or Control-click) the app and choose "Open", then click
    "Open" again in the dialog that appears. This only needs to be done once.
  - If that doesn't show an "Open" option, go to System Settings > Privacy &
    Security, scroll down to the note about this app being blocked, and click
    "Open Anyway".

Uninstalling
------------
Drag the app to the Trash. To also remove your saved data (profile,
connections, cached content), see Settings > "Delete local app data" inside
the app first, or manually remove:
  ~/Library/Application Support/ai-office-front-desk
